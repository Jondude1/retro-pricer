RETRO PRICER — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Retro Pricer ("the app") is a price-lookup tool for retro video games. This page explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Account & personal information

Retro Pricer does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.

What the app sends over the network

When you search for a game, the app sends your search text and selected console filter to our server (retro-pricer.onrender.com) to look up pricing data. No other data is transmitted.

Data storage

We do not store search history tied to an individual user or device. Aggregate, non-identifying lookup counts may be kept for service reliability purposes only.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.

Permissions

The app requests standard internet access only, required to fetch pricing data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach the developer via the contact details listed on the Google Play Store listing for Retro Pricer.
